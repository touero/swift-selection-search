  • How to customize something?
    Click Chrome's Extensions icon (the puzzle piece) in the toolbar, then click the three-dot menu next to Swift Selection Search and choose Options. You can also open chrome://extensions, click Details on this extension, and then Extension options.
  • How to add new search engines? Or remove some?
    You can do that in the Options page as well. :) Be sure to read the instructions at the top!
  • Where is the popup / how do I pin SSS?
    Click the Extensions icon (the puzzle piece) in Chrome's toolbar and use the pin button next to Swift Selection Search. The toolbar icon opens SSS's popup.
  • Found a problem?
    In the Options page please expand the "Instructions" section and check "Problems?".
